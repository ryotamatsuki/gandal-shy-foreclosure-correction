\begin{abstract}
This note revisits the post-foreclosure price equilibrium in Gandal and Shy (2001). Their published model uses quadratic transportation costs and correctly identifies $c=5/2$ as the conversion-cost threshold at which an outsider is foreclosed from a standardization-union member market. Re-solving the two-active-firm subgame changes the subsequent price characterization. Member firms charge $p_M=c-1$ for $5/2<c<3$, where the foreclosure constraint binds and pricing is limit pricing, and $p_M=2$ for $3\leq c<5$, the unconstrained duopoly price. We then recompute member-country welfare. Despite the equilibrium correction, the welfare ranking in their Proposition 3 remains valid because price payments by consumers and member-firm revenues offset in national welfare.
\end{abstract}

# C5 Journal Requirements — International Economics

Access date: 2026-08-30.

Primary source: Elsevier / ScienceDirect, *International Economics — Guide for Authors*:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-economics/publish/guide-for-authors

## Article type and short-paper constraints

- The journal describes an additional submission form as a **Short communication**, while the same Guide labels the length-limited section **Short paper**. The exact article-type label shown in the live submission portal should therefore be confirmed at C6.
- Short papers are capped at **7,000 words**.
- Each table or figure reduces the permitted word count by 200 words.
- A maximum of five exhibits is permitted.
- The manuscript must be **self-contained**. The Guide states that a paper can be rejected if its contribution cannot be assessed from the main text without consulting appendices.
- The current manuscript has no exhibits and is far below the word ceiling.

## Review model and current journal statistics

- Peer review is **single anonymized**.
- Suitable submissions are normally sent to at least two reviewers.
- The Guide currently reports 39 days to first decision and an approximate acceptance rate of 12%.

## Initial-submission files and formatting

- Editable source files are required; `.tex` is explicitly accepted and a PDF is not an acceptable source file by itself.
- The title page must include author names, affiliations, and a corresponding author with current contact details.
- Abstract maximum: **250 words**.
- Required keywords: **1–6**, in English.
- Highlights are **encouraged**, not stated as mandatory: 3–5 bullets, maximum 85 characters each, uploaded as a separate editable file.
- The current Guide does not state a JEL-code requirement. JEL codes may be retained if useful, but they are not treated as a verified requirement.
- References may be submitted in any consistent format initially; DOIs are encouraged and journal style is applied after acceptance.

## Declarations and transparency

- A competing-interests declaration is required through Elsevier's declarations workflow; authors with nothing to disclose select the corresponding no-interest option and upload the generated `.doc/.docx` declaration.
- Funding sources must be disclosed. If there was no specific funding, the Guide recommends the standard no-funding sentence.
- **Generative-AI use must be declared** when AI tools were used in manuscript preparation. The declaration is placed in a dedicated section before the references at first submission. The author remains responsible for verification and final content.
- Corresponding authors are required to provide a CRediT contribution statement.

## Code, data, and supplementary files

- The journal follows its research-data **Option B**: authors are encouraged to deposit research materials, cite/link them, and provide an availability statement. The policy explicitly includes software, code, models, and algorithms within research data.
- A data-availability statement is encouraged.
- Supplementary material is allowed, must be submitted with the manuscript, cited in the manuscript, and will be posted substantially as received.
- For this correction note, C5 recommends that the proof be entirely self-contained in the main manuscript and that the verification code be handled through a code/data-availability statement rather than a technical appendix containing secondary corrections.

## Fees and access model

- The current *International Economics* Guide for Authors contains **no stated submission fee**. Elsevier's general submission-fee policy says journals that charge a submission fee flag it in the journal Guide and during submission. C6 should nevertheless confirm the live portal before payment-sensitive submission steps.
- The journal supports open access. The Guide notes that an APC applies if the author chooses open access and the article is accepted.
- Elsevier distinguishes Gold OA from subscription publication; its author guidance states that no author publication fee is payable under the subscription route because costs are covered by subscriptions. The exact current OA APC is not needed for the standard subscription route and should be checked in the live journal/author portal if OA is considered.

## C5 compliance conclusion

The current short note is comfortably within the verified Short-paper ceiling, has no exhibits, is self-contained, and can be supplied as editable LaTeX. C6 must add the administrative submission package: real title-page metadata, corresponding-author information, AI declaration, funding and competing-interest declarations, CRediT, code/data availability statement, and (optionally) highlights.

# Source Ledger

No publisher or working-paper PDF is committed to this repository.

## Published target article

Neil Gandal and Oz Shy (2001), "Standardization policy and international trade," *Journal of International Economics*, 53(2), 363-383, April 2001. DOI: `10.1016/S0022-1996(00)00067-2`.

Primary-source points used in the manuscript:

- pp. 367-368: Salop circle with circumference 3, uniformly distributed consumers, and quadratic transportation disutility `[s_i(x)]^2`;
- Section 3.2 and Proposition 3: standardization-union foreclosure and member-country welfare comparison;
- Appendix B, pp. 381-382: published post-foreclosure price characterization and long-arc calculation;
- Appendix C, p. 382: welfare accounting used to prove Proposition 3.

Official metadata: Elsevier / ScienceDirect article record and DOI above.

## Earlier working-paper version

Neil Gandal and Oz Shy (1996), "Standardization Policy and International Trade," Working Paper No. 12-96, Tel Aviv University, Foerder Institute for Economic Research. DOI: `10.22004/ag.econ.275615`.

AgEcon Search record: `https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/275615`.

This version is used only for the restrained specification-history footnote: it uses linear transportation costs, unlike the quadratic transportation disutility in the 2001 published article.

Steven C. Salop (1979), "Monopolistic Competition with Outside Goods," *Bell Journal of Economics*, 10(1), 141-156.

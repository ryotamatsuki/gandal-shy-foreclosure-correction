- The 1996 working-paper version is *Standardization Policy and International Trade*, Working Paper No. 12-96, Tel Aviv University / Foerder Institute for Economic Research, DOI `10.22004/ag.econ.275615`.
- Earlier working-paper versions use linear transportation costs. Under that technology the corresponding long-arc formula and the symmetric two-firm price `3 tau / 2` are internally consistent.
- The 2001 *Journal of International Economics* article uses quadratic transportation disutility.
- The earlier working papers do not contain the C1 corrected equilibrium for the published quadratic specification.

The genealogy is used only to distinguish specifications. It is not evidence about author intent or the production history of the published derivation.

## Primary contribution

## Exactly two main propositions

### Proposition 1

For `5/2<c<5`, the outsider is foreclosed, member quantities are `3/2`, and

- `p_M=c-1` for `5/2<c<3`;
- `p_M=2` for `3<=c<5`.

### Proposition 2

Under the recognition behavior used in Gandal and Shy Proposition 3,

- `TS_M^SU=3V+1/4`;
- `TS^MR=3V-1/4`;
- the welfare difference is `1/2>0`.

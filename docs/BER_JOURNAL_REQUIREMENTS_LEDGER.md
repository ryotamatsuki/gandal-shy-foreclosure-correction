**Checked:** 2026-10-01 JST

## Confirmed from the official guide

- Refereed short articles, notes and comments are eligible formats. Exact portal label is unverified.
- Originality, substantive contribution and wider economist interest remain editorial requirements.
- Abstract: at most 150 words. Include keywords and JEL codes.
- ORCID and applicable declarations are requested.
- LaTeX submissions require a review PDF and supporting source files.
- Submission uses Wiley Authors. Simultaneous journal consideration is prohibited.
- Standard publication and optional OA routes are described.

These facts establish format eligibility, not acceptance prospects.

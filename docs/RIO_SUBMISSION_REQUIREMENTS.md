## Editorial fit

- RIO publishes research on all aspects of industrial organization, broadly defined.
- It explicitly seeks papers that advance significant theories of industrial organization and policy.
- The Editors explicitly welcome shorter notes and commentaries on topics of interest to the profession.
- This makes a concise correction of a published oligopoly equilibrium a format-compatible submission, subject to editorial significance.

## Submission system and files

- Submission is through Springer Nature's online submission system (`submission.nature.com` / `submission.springernature.com`).
- Editable source files are required at submission and revision.
- Mathematical manuscripts may be submitted in LaTeX.
- LaTeX submissions must not use subfolders; all source, bibliography, and related files should be at one folder level.
- Uploaded LaTeX sources are automatically compiled into a PDF for author approval and peer review.

## Manuscript metadata and structure

- Title must be concise and informative.
- Title page: author name, affiliation, city/state/country as applicable, corresponding-author email, and ORCID if available.
- Abstract: 150–250 words.
- Keywords: 4–6.
- JEL classification codes are required in an appropriate number.
- Number pages automatically.
- Use decimal heading hierarchy with no more than three levels where hierarchical headings are used.
- Acknowledgments, if any, belong in a separate title-page section.

## Statements and Declarations

- The manuscript must contain a `Statements and Declarations` section.
- Competing interests must be disclosed; related information must also be supplied through the submission interface.
- All original research articles must contain a Data Availability Statement.
- For this theoretical note, the statement records that no empirical data were used and that symbolic/numerical verification scripts are supplied as Supplementary Information.

## Generative AI / LLM policy

- LLMs cannot be authors.
- Substantive LLM use should be documented in the Methods section or, if no Methods section exists, in a suitable alternative part of the manuscript.
- Pure AI-assisted copy editing does not require disclosure, but the present project involved substantive assistance beyond copy editing (source searching, drafting support, algebraic cross-checking, and verification-code drafting), so the manuscript includes an explicit disclosure under `Statements and Declarations`.
- Human accountability remains with the author; all sources, mathematics, numerical checks, and final text must be independently verified.

## References

- Author-year citation style.
- Reference list alphabetized by first-author surname.
- Only cited works should appear in the reference list.
- Full DOI links should be included when available.

## Supplementary Information

- Supplementary files are permitted and are published substantially as received.
- The verification scripts will be supplied as a reproducibility supplement.

## Costs

- RIO is a hybrid journal.
- Subscription publishing is available and **no APC charges apply** under that route.
- Optional open access currently carries an APC of GBP 2,390 / USD 3,290 / EUR 2,690, determined by the acceptance date.
- No submission fee is stated on the current official RIO aims/scope, submission-guidelines, or fees pages. The live submission portal should nevertheless be checked immediately before final submission; if any submission fee appears, stop before payment and reassess.

## RIO package decision

Target publishing route: **subscription publishing**.

Target manuscript form: concise self-contained IO note, taking advantage of RIO's explicit openness to shorter notes and commentaries without assuming a separate portal article-type label unless the live portal provides one.

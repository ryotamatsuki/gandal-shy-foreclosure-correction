## Verified requirements

- Submission platform: Wiley Research Exchange.
- Free Format submission is available.
- Main manuscript may be submitted as LaTeX (`.tex`). A PDF version must also be uploaded for peer review, and files referred to by the LaTeX main document should be uploaded as LaTeX supplementary files.
- Manuscript maximum: 25 double-spaced typewritten pages.
- Review model: single anonymized; papers sent out are peer reviewed by at least two anonymous reviewers.
- Title should be short and informative and contain major keywords.
- Abstract may be structured or unstructured.
- Up to seven keywords.
- Practitioner Points are optional, maximum three. They are deliberately omitted for this narrowly theoretical correction because forced practitioner claims would overstate the paper.
- ORCID is required.
- The title page should contain author details and relevant ethics/integrity statements, including data availability, funding, and conflict-of-interest disclosure where applicable.
- All accepted manuscripts require a data availability statement. No empirical data are used here; analytical derivations are in the article and verification scripts are supplied as supporting information.
- Wiley requires disclosure of substantive AI use and human verification. The manuscript contains a dedicated AI use declaration.
- Submission fee: CHF 100 on first submission for all authors; it is not refunded after desk rejection. Revisions do not incur another submission fee.
- Open access is optional. If it is not selected, the subscription/copyright-transfer route remains available; no optional OA APC is required under that route. If OA is selected, the current Wiley journal page should be checked again immediately before acceptance/payment.
- Papers are submitted on the understanding that their substance has not been published and they are not under consideration elsewhere.

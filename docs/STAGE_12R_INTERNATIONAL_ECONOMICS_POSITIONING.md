## 3. Frozen contribution entering Stage 12R

The paper continues to make exactly two substantive claims:

1. In the published quadratic-transport specification of Gandal and Shy (2001), the post-foreclosure price equilibrium is piecewise: for `5/2<c<3`, member firms set `p_M=c-1` under a binding exclusion constraint; for `3<=c<5`, the unconstrained symmetric-duopoly price `p_M=2` obtains. After foreclosure, `q_1=q_2=3/2` and `q_3=0`.
2. Recomputing member-country welfare under the corrected equilibrium leaves the Proposition 3 ranking unchanged: `TS_M^SU=3V+1/4`, `TS^MR=3V-1/4`, and the gap remains `1/2`.

The economic interpretation is also frozen: realized zero sales do not necessarily make the excluded outsider strategically irrelevant, because a price increase by active firms can restore outsider demand.

## 5. Current evidence for International Economics

### 5.1 Scope and article type

Current official journal page, accessed 2026-09-09:

- https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/international-economics/2110-7017

The journal describes itself as publishing original research in applied international economics, including trade and trade policy. It states that empirical work is especially welcome, but it does not define the journal as empirical-only.

The same current page identifies additional submission forms including `Short communication`, while the length-limited section is described as `Short paper`. The short-paper rules are:

- at most 7,000 words;
- each table or figure reduces the word limit by 200 words;
- at most five exhibits;
- the manuscript must be self-contained;
- the editor/referees must be able to assess the contribution from the main text without relying on appendices.

The current manuscript is comfortably within this format and has no substantive appendix dependency.

## 7. Primary-target rationale

**Primary target: International Economics, short-format route.**

This is the best current match under the zero-fee constraint because:

1. the institutional object is international product standards, recognition policy, a standardization union, and an outside-country firm;
2. the corrected price equilibrium changes the strategic implementation of the standardization policy benchmark even though the member-country welfare ranking survives;
3. the source paper is a recognized international-economics benchmark;
4. the short-format route explicitly accommodates a self-contained manuscript of this size;
5. recent journal content confirms an audience for theoretical work on standards and global economic interaction;
6. the RIO editorial outcome indicates that the principal problem was IO-reader fit rather than technical correctness.

The primary residual risk is **editorial significance**, not mathematics: an editor may conclude that a correction to a 2001 benchmark is too narrow because the headline welfare comparison remains intact.

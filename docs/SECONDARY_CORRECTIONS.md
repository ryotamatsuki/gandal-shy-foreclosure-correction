# Secondary consistency corrections — repository record only

These results were identified during C0–C4 but are deliberately excluded from the submitted main manuscript after C5 triage. They are not additional contributions or propositions of the Short Paper.

## Table 2 arithmetic

For the first conversion-cost row of Table 2, direct recomputation gives member-country surplus

\[
3V+\frac{20c^2-60c-25}{100},
\]

rather than the published coefficient `16c^2`. The difference is `c^2/25`. This row is off equilibrium for the outsider's recognition decision and does not change the paper's main equilibrium comparison.

## Country-size calculation

The member firm's per-member-market profit coefficient must be piecewise once foreclosure occurs:

\[
m(c)=
\begin{cases}
(c+5)^2/25, & 0<c<5/2,\\
(3/2)(c-1), & 5/2<c<3,\\
3, & c\ge 3.
\end{cases}
\]

The qualitative larger-partner ranking survives because `m(c)>1` over the relevant positive-`c` regions.

## Network-effect analogue

The same quadratic long-arc geometry changes the post-foreclosure price calculation in the network-effect analogue. The corresponding member price is `p_M=d-1` for `5/2<d<3` and `p_M=2` for `d>=3`. The original qualitative mutual-recognition welfare comparison survives.

## Boundary and outsider-price details

The main manuscript now contains all proof-relevant boundary facts. At `c=5/2`, the limit-pricing branch joins the three-active-firm limit at `p_M=3/2`. At `c=3`, the two post-foreclosure branches join at `p_M=2`. For sufficiently high `c`, a zero-demand outsider's price need not be unique; the main proposition therefore characterizes the member prices and allocation rather than an arbitrary full zero-sales price vector.
